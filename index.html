01

Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Pick and Play mobile app ,available on Android and iOS. It's written for anyone using the app to learn chords, practice tuning, watch lesson content, or manage a Pick and Play account.

By using Pick and Play, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

02

Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to run your lessons, track your progress, and keep your account secure.

CategoryWhat it includesWhy we collect it
Account info Phone number, one-time verification code, and any name or profile photo you add Creating your account and signing you back in
Microphone audio Live audio captured while a tuner or pitch-practice screen is open Detecting pitch and timing to give you real-time feedback
Practice & usage data Chords and lessons completed, scores, streaks, screens visited, app crashes Tracking your progress and fixing bugs
Payment QR data A generated bank-transfer QR code, saved to your photo library only if you tap "Save" Letting you pay for a subscription using your own banking app
Device & notification data Device type, OS version, and a push-notification token Sending reminders and keeping the app stable across devices
Microphone audio is processed on your device to detect pitch — we do not upload or store your raw recordings. The app only asks for camera-roll access to add a payment QR code image to your library; it does not read your existing photos.
03

How we use your information

  • Create, verify, and secure your Pick and Play account
  • Give you real-time pitch and tuning feedback during practice
  • Save your practice history, streaks, and lesson progress
  • Generate and process QR codes for subscription payments
  • Send you reminders, lesson updates, and service announcements
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve the app's performance
  • Respond to support requests you send us
04

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to run parts of the app. We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Push notifications — delivered through OneSignal, which receives a device token so it can route messages to your device.
  • Lesson video playback — embedded YouTube players load videos directly from YouTube, which applies Google's own privacy practices to that playback.
  • Bank transfers — QR payment codes are scanned by your own banking app; Pick and Play does not receive or store your card or bank login details.

These providers are only permitted to use your data to deliver their service to us — not for their own advertising.

05

Storage & security

Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure, encrypted storage. Preferences and cached lesson data are kept in local app storage on your device. Account and progress data that needs to sync across your devices is sent, over an encrypted connection, to our servers.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we apply industry-standard safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

06

Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run the app (see Third-party services), under obligations to protect it
  • If required by law, court order, or a valid government request
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Pick and Play, our users, or the public
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you
07

Data retention

We keep your account and practice data for as long as your account is active, so your progress carries over between sessions and devices. Microphone audio used for pitch detection is processed live and not retained after the analysis is complete. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep records for legal or accounting purposes.

08

Your choices

  • Microphone access — can be turned off any time in your device settings; pitch-detection features simply won't work without it.
  • Photo library access — only needed if you save a payment QR code; you can decline and pay another way instead.
  • Notifications — can be disabled from your device settings or the app's notification preferences.
  • Access, correct, or delete your data — email us at greentechnology.cambodia@gmail.com and we'll help you review, update, or close your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to data portability and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

09

Children's privacy

Pick and Play is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10

International users

Your information may be processed and stored in a country other than where you live. Wherever it's processed, we apply the same protections described in this policy.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. If we make a material change, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continuing to use Pick and Play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
